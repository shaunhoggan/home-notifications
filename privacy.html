Privacy Policy

Home Notifications

Effective date: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

Home Notifications is a private infrastructure notification service. It delivers operational messages from authorized applications to designated recipients.

2. Information Processed

The service may process recipient email addresses, recipient identifiers, notification subjects, message content, severity classifications, timestamps, and delivery-status information.

Operational messages may contain infrastructure health information, diagnostic summaries, or other information submitted by authorized systems.

3. Google API Access

Home Notifications uses the Gmail API with the following OAuth permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

This permission allows the application to send messages on behalf of the Google account that explicitly authorizes the service.

Home Notifications does not request permission to read, modify, or delete mailbox messages.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to validate notification requests, determine authorized destinations, construct messages, submit emails for delivery, manage retries, and maintain operational delivery records.

Google authorization is used only to provide the email-sending functionality of this service.

5. Information Sharing

Information necessary to send email is transmitted to Google's Gmail API for message delivery. Messages are also delivered to their designated recipients.

Home Notifications does not sell personal data or use Google user data for advertising.

6. Storage and Security

The notification service uses access controls and recipient authorization rules. Application credentials and Google OAuth tokens are maintained separately from public source code.

Notification and delivery records may be retained for operational troubleshooting, auditing, and reliability purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Notification records are retained according to the service's operational retention configuration. Authorized operators may request removal of retained recipient information or notification records, subject to applicable operational requirements.

8. Google Authorization Revocation

The account holder may revoke the application's authorization through their Google Account security settings.

Revocation prevents future authorized Gmail API access until access is granted again. It does not automatically delete previously retained notification records.

9. Access and Intended Users

This service is intended for private operational use. It is not a public email marketing service or an open notification delivery platform.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service's functionality, security practices, or information handling changes.
